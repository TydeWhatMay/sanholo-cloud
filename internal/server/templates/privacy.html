What SanHolo Hosted stores

Waiting list and invites

Your email address and the date you joined the waiting list. An invite stores your email address, its expiry date and a hash of the invite code. The database does not hold the code. The email that carries the code is queued until it is sent, and its text is then erased.

Your account

Your email address and the date the account was created. A hash of the address is stored beside it for sign-in lookups.

Signing in

  • A sign-in request stores only a hash of the email address entered.
  • A sign-in link is stored as a hash of its token. It expires after 15 minutes and works once. The email that carries the link is queued until it is sent, and its text is then erased.
  • Each signed-in session stores a hash of the session cookie, the browser's user agent text, how the session was opened (email link or passkey), and when it was created and last used. A session ends 30 days after it was last used, or earlier when you sign out or revoke it on the account page.
  • A passkey stores its public key, its credential identifier, a signature counter and the dates it was added and last used. The private key stays on your device.

Your game account

To play for you, SanHolo needs your Replicant Space API token. The token is stored encrypted in a secrets store on SanHolo's servers. Only your own SanHolo instance can read it. The sign-up service can write and delete it but cannot read it back. The token is never written to the database, to logs or to the export.

The database records which game account you connected: an identifier derived from the game account's earliest replicant, the name and code of that replicant, and the date you connected it. SanHolo uses the identifier to refuse a game account that is already connected to another SanHolo account. To check the token, SanHolo asks the game once for your account's replicants; it keeps nothing else the game returns, and not your game email address. Between checking a token and your confirmation, the token is held in the server's memory for up to 10 minutes and then discarded.

When you replace your token, the secrets store keeps the earlier token as an older version. It keeps up to 16 versions and drops the oldest. Older versions stay until you disconnect your game account or delete your account.

Disconnecting your game account stops your SanHolo instance and deletes your token, your instance volume and the record of the game account. If the secrets store or the disk volume cannot complete a deletion at once, SanHolo records it and retries until it succeeds. Your SanHolo account stays.

Your SanHolo instance stores the game state it reads from Replicant Space, the bot's activity log, your goals and settings, on a disk volume reserved for your account.

Notifications

If you turn on browser notifications, SanHolo stores the push subscription your browser provides for each browser you enable: the push service address of your browser's vendor, two keys that belong to that subscription, the date it was made, and which of your sessions made it. An account can hold up to 10 subscriptions. SanHolo sends at most 30 notifications an hour per account.

A notification carries the alert title, the alert text from your SanHolo instance (up to 240 characters) and a link to your Bridge. Notifications are delivered through the push service of your browser's vendor. The content is encrypted on SanHolo's server with your browser's keys, so the push service passes it on without being able to read it. The push service does see that a notification was sent to your browser, and when.

Email sent to you

Invites, sign-in links and notices that a passkey was added are queued before sending. The text of a queued email is erased when the email is sent or given up on. The queue entry is deleted 7 days after that.

Technical data

Rate limits on the waiting list, sign-up, sign-in and passkey forms are keyed on your IP address (for IPv6, the network it belongs to) and, for sign-in, on a hash of the email address entered. These records are deleted after 2 days.

Server logs record the time, the page requested, the response status and the IP address of each request. They are kept for [log retention to be set at launch].

Administrator actions

Every action an administrator takes (issuing an invite, suspending an account) is recorded with the time and the administrator. An invite is recorded by its internal identifier, not by the email address. The identifier links to nothing once the invite is deleted.

What SanHolo Hosted does not collect

SanHolo Hosted runs no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts. The pages load scripts and styles from sanholo.space only, and the content security policy refuses everything else. Data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Account, sign-in, your SanHolo instance and notifications: to provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Waiting list and invites: to let you into the beta at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Rate limits, server logs and the administrator record: to keep the service available and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long data is kept

  • Waiting list entry: until you create an account, or until you ask for it to be removed.
  • Invites: 14 days after they expire. An invite is valid for 14 days.
  • Account, game account record, game token and instance volume: until you delete your account or disconnect your game account.
  • Push subscriptions: until you turn notifications off, until your browser's push service reports the subscription as gone, or until you delete your account. A subscription stays when you sign out.
  • Sessions: 30 days after last use.
  • Sign-in links and requests: 1 day after use or expiry.
  • Queued email: the text is erased when the email is sent or given up on. The queue entry is deleted 7 days after that.
  • Rate-limit records: 2 days.
  • Backups: encrypted nightly backups of the database and instance volumes are kept for 30 days. Data you delete remains in backups taken before the deletion until they expire, at most 30 days, and in the secrets store's internal log until it is compacted. A deleted account is not restored from a backup.
  • Administrator record: kept while the service runs. It holds no email addresses, no hashes of them and no tokens.

Export and deletion

The account page offers an export of your data as a ZIP file. The download link works once and expires after 24 hours. The export never contains your game token.

Deleting your account stops your SanHolo instance, deletes your game token from the secrets store, deletes your instance volume and deletes every database record belonging to your account. Deletion is permanent.
