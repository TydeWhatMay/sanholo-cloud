Deleting your account stops your bot, deletes your game token from the secrets store, deletes your bot's data volume and deletes every record SanHolo holds for your account: your email address, sessions, passkeys, notification subscriptions, game account record, invites and export links.

The deletion is permanent. SanHolo cannot restore the account afterwards. Export your data first if you want a copy.

Your Replicant Space account is not deleted. Delete it in the game if you want it gone.

Encrypted backups made before the deletion keep your data until they expire, at most 30 days. A deleted account is not restored from a backup.

If the secrets store or the disk volume cannot complete a deletion at once, SanHolo records it and retries until it succeeds. The confirmation says when that happens.
